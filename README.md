A single line of text, 1 to 240 characters, that the Moon says back to
everyone who has ever stared up at it — an answer to the wishes, the howls
and the late-night promises.

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-moon-finally-answers-back-781a7ec1).

## How it works

`main.py` writes the Moon's comeback to `/output/entry.txt`. One line,
deterministic, no network, no secrets.
